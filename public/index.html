Quick Resources

Email Tips for Biotech Students

How to effectively communicate with professors and industry professionals

  1. Address professors with their proper titles and mention specific biotech research interests
  2. Include relevant coursework or lab experience in your introduction
  3. Be specific about which biotech research papers or projects you're referencing
  4. Use proper scientific terminology and avoid jargon when unnecessary
  5. Request specific information about research opportunities or lab positions

SOP Tips for Biotech Programs

Guidelines for crafting a compelling Statement of Purpose for biotech programs

  1. Highlight specific biotech laboratory techniques you've mastered
  2. Discuss relevant research experience in molecular biology, genetics, or biochemistry
  3. Explain your interest in specific biotech subfields (e.g., genetic engineering, bioinformatics)
  4. Connect your academic background to current biotech industry challenges
  5. Mention specific faculty members whose research aligns with your interests
  6. Include any publications, presentations, or biotech competitions you've participated in
