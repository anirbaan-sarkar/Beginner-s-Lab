Mastering Professional Communication in BioTech

Effective email communication is crucial in academia and industry. Find tips tailored for BioTech contexts below. These will be updated periodically!

Writing a Professional Email Subject Line

Your subject line is the first impression. Be clear and concise. Examples: "Inquiry about Research Opportunity - [Your Name]", "Question Regarding [Course Name] Assignment", "Application for [Position Name] - [Your Name]". Avoid vague subjects like "Hi" or "Question".

Email Template: Requesting an Informational Interview

When reaching out to someone in industry: Start with a polite greeting, introduce yourself briefly (student, area of interest), state why you're contacting *them* specifically (their work, company), clearly ask for a brief informational interview (15-20 mins), offer flexibility, and thank them for their time.

Following Up Politely

If you haven't heard back after a reasonable time (e.g., a week for non-urgent matters), a polite follow-up is okay. Forward your original email and add a brief note like: "Dear [Name], I hope this email finds you well. I'm gently following up on my previous message regarding [briefly state topic]. I understand you're busy, but would appreciate any information you might have time to share. Best regards, [Your Name]".
